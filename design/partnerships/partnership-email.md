# Partnership outreach email

Cold/warm outreach to **Hibiscus Coast businesses and organisations that already
run an email list or an engaged audience** — gyms, clinics, real-estate offices,
garden centres, cafés with a loyalty list, schools and preschools, sports clubs,
the surf club, community groups, other local newsletters. The ask is a
**mutual promotion**: they tell their people about The Tide, The Tide puts them
in front of Coasties. No money moves.

Contact address for all of this: `hello@thetide.co.nz`. Site: `thetide.co.nz`.

---

## How the swap actually works (the offer)

Pick from this menu per partner — lead with the first two, hold the rest for the
follow-up or the "let's make it regular" conversation:

| We give the partner | They give us |
| --- | --- |
| A written feature in an issue — a real write-up, not a banner | One mention of The Tide to their list, with our copy + signup link |
| A standing spot in the [Hibiscus Coast business directory](https://thetide.co.nz/hibiscus-coast-business-directory) | A signup link in their newsletter footer / website |
| A comped event or classified listing when they have something on | A shout-out on their social once |
| "Founding local partner" wording, capped at a handful | An intro to one or two other local operators who'd want in |
| A recurring monthly shout-out, a cross-post, or a joint giveaway once it's proven | The same back |

**Compliance — this is the important bit.** The partner emails *their own list
from their own account*. We never receive, upload, or hold their list. Every new
reader signs up themselves at `thetide.co.nz`. That keeps both sides inside the
Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007 — it's an endorsed opt-in, not a list
swap. Say so in the email; a few partners will ask.

## Subject lines

Test a couple. Shorter and plainer wins on the Coast.

- `A local swap — The Tide × {{business}}`
- `Cross-promo idea for {{business}}?`

## Personalising

Fill these before sending — a mail-merge with an unfilled `{{business}}` in it
is an instant delete:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| `{{first_name}}` | The owner or whoever runs their comms. Find a real name; "Hi there" halves the reply rate. |
| `{{business}}` | Their trading name as customers know it. |
| One specific line | Best if you can add a sentence that proves you know them — a recent opening, an event they ran, something they posted. Drop it in after the first paragraph. |
| Send day | The Tide goes out weekly — name the day once it's fixed so "every week" can become "every {{day}}". |

**List size to work:** 15–30 of these per market push, sent in small batches
over a fortnight, not all at once. Track replies and which *type* of business
says yes — that's what tells you who to approach first next time.
