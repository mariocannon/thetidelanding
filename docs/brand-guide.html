02 — Signature elements

The gradient and the tide

Two ownable devices carry the brand. A soft radial wash from Foam into Sand, and a pair of Sea Glass waves anchored to the bottom of the page. Reproduce them exactly.

Background wash

A radial gradient centred at the top of the page. Keep it subtle — the shift is felt more than seen.

background: radial-gradient( 120% 90% at 50% 0%, #faf5ea 0%, #f0e7d6 60% );

The tide

Two layered waves in Sea Glass — the back wave at 45% opacity, the front at full. Fixed to the foot of the viewport.

fill: #a2c5d3 back wave → opacity 0.45 front wave → opacity 1.0

03 — Typography

Native type, on purpose

The Tide ships no web fonts — it uses the reader's own system UI stack so the page loads instantly and feels at home on every device. Personality comes from scale, weight and the uppercase, wide-tracked eyebrow.

Eyebrow The Coast's newsletter
Display / H1 A newsletter worth waiting for
Heading / H2 The week's local stories
Lede Hundreds of Coasties get the week's news delivered every week.
Body The Tide is a weekly email bringing local stories, news and events — sign up with just your email, free forever.
Note / caption Free forever. Unsubscribe any time.
font-family: system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif; eyebrow → 700 · uppercase · letter-spacing 0.18em display → 800 · letter-spacing -0.02em · text-wrap: balance

04 — Components

The signup, in the flesh

The whole product is one form. These are the real controls — same colours, radii and focus behaviour as production. Tab into the field to see the Sea Glass focus ring; hover the button for its Steel Blue lift.

Free forever. Unsubscribe any time. Please enter a valid email address. You're in. Watch your inbox.

Button

Sea Glass fill, Deep Harbor label, 0.65rem radius. Hover lifts 1px with a Steel Blue shadow at 35% — never changes colour.

Input

Paper surface, Deep Navy border at 25%. On focus the border goes Steel Blue with a 3px Sea Glass glow at 55%.

Status notes

Driftwood by default, Coral for errors, Kelp for success. Kelp success text also steps up in size to celebrate.

05 — Voice & tone

Warm, local, unhurried

The copy speaks to "Coasties" like a neighbour, not a marketer. Short, friendly, and never pushy — every line reassures more than it sells.

Welcoming

"You're in good company!"

Belonging before benefit. The reader is joining neighbours, not a mailing list.

Reassuring

"Free forever. Unsubscribe any time."

Removes friction up front. No fine print, no catch, no pressure.

Human on error

"Something went wrong — please try again in a moment."

Owns the problem plainly, points to the fix, skips the apology spiral.

Celebratory

"You're in. Watch your inbox for the next issue."

Confirms success and sets the next expectation in one warm breath.

  • Coastal, not corporate. Lean on the tide, the shore and "Coasties" — the identity is a place, not a product.
  • Calm over loud. Soft grounds, one gentle accent, generous space. Nothing shouts for attention.
  • Fast and light. No web fonts, no external CSS or images beyond the logo — the brand's speed is part of its promise.
  • One clear action. Everything points to a single email field. Never add a competing call to action.
