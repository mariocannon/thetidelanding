# Social posts — partnership call-out

For The Tide's own channels. The audience here is **local business owners who
already follow the page** (a lot of them do) — the post is an open invitation to
cross-promote. Pair the feed posts with `thetide-partner-invite-1080x1080.png`;
the link/LinkedIn posts with `thetide-partner-invite-wide-1200x630.png`.

Address in words on every post — an image isn't clickable everywhere it lands:
**hello@thetide.co.nz**.

---

## 1. Facebook — main post (with the 1080×1080 image)

> **Run a business on the Coast? Let's team up.** 🌊
>
> The Tide goes out to a few hundred locals every week — Ōrewa, Whangaparāoa,
> Silverdale, Red Beach. If you've got a mailing list, a shopfront full of
> regulars, or an audience of your own, we'd love to cross-promote.
>
> Here's the deal: you give us one mention to your people, we feature your
> business in an issue and list you in the Hibiscus Coast business directory.
> Free both ways. No lists change hands — folks just sign up themselves.
>
> Keen? Message the page or email hello@thetide.co.nz 👋

## Notes

- **Cadence:** run posts 1–3 once, spaced a week or two apart, when you're
  actively doing an outreach push — not on a loop. Post 6 goes out each time a
  partnership starts.
- **Keep the ask concrete.** "Let's team up" with no detail gets likes, not
  emails. Every post names the swap and the address.
- **Don't overpromise the reach.** "A few hundred" until the number genuinely
  moves — the billboard's "hundreds of Coasties" is the ceiling of the current
  claim.
- Macronised spellings in captions — Ōrewa, Whangaparāoa — matching the site.
  Hashtags stay ASCII (`#Whangaparaoa`).
